Shipping

Ready-to-wear pieces are dispatched within two working days. Bespoke commissions are collected from the atelier by appointment, or shipped on request once the final fitting is signed off.

Returns

Ready-to-wear may be returned within 14 days of delivery, unworn, unwashed and with tags attached. Write to the atelier first so we can issue a return reference; unannounced returns are difficult to trace.

Bespoke commissions cannot be returned. They are cut to your measurements from a pattern drafted for you. What we do instead is fit twice before finishing, and alter free of charge afterwards for as long as you own the garment.

Accessories are returnable unless they have been strung or sized to order.

Garment care

Embroidered and ceremonial pieces are dry clean only, and should go to a cleaner who has handled metallic thread before. Ask them; a good one will tell you honestly.

Store ceremonial garments folded with acid-free tissue between the embroidered panels, never on a hanger — the weight of the cloth will distort the shoulder over a year on a hook.

Cotton kaftans and senators improve with washing. Cold, gentle, inside out, line dried in shade. Press while slightly damp.

Payment

This website is a frontend demonstration. No payment processor is connected to it, no order can be placed through it, and no card details are collected anywhere on this site. The bag and checkout exist to show the shopping experience only.

In the atelier, commissions are taken with a 50% deposit at the first appointment and the balance on collection. We accept bank transfer and card in person.

Ready-to-wear is paid in full at the point of order.
