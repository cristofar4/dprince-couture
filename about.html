The house

We make fourteen ceremonial agbada a year

That number is not a position. It is the honest capacity of one embroidery bench, and it decides everything else about how this house works.

Since 2014

Beginning

The house began with a commission it was not equipped to take: a full ceremonial ensemble, four pieces, eleven weeks, for a chieftaincy ceremony in Ọ̀yọ́.

It was delivered three days late and the shoulder was wrong. The client wore it anyway, and afterwards explained precisely what was wrong with it — the drape collapsed when he raised his arm to greet people, which is the single thing a man does most at his own ceremony.

That conversation is the reason this house exists in the form it does. Every garment since has been fitted twice, in a basted state that looks deliberately unfinished, so that the wearer can find what is wrong while it can still be changed.

How we work

Three commitments

Cloth with a source

Aso-oke is bought from four weaving families in Ìsẹ̀yìn, ordered a season ahead at a price the weavers set. The narrow loom decides our motif sizes, not the other way round.

Construction you cannot see

Seams bound rather than overlocked. Embroidery on the ground cloth rather than an applied panel. None of this photographs. All of it matters in 2046.

A pace we do not compress

Ten to fourteen weeks for a ceremonial commission. We do not shorten it for a fee, because the compression would come out of the fittings — the part you are actually paying for.

The commission

Four stages, ten to fourteen weeks

01

First appointment — 90 minutes

Twenty-two measurement points, taken twice. Cloth selected. A conversation about the occasion, because a coronation garment and a wedding garment are different objects.

02

Pattern and first cut — 3 weeks

A paper pattern drafted specifically to you, then filed under your name. Every later commission starts from it rather than from a block.

03

First fitting — the honest one

Presented basted, tacked in contrast thread, deliberately unfinished. It will look wrong. That is the point: this is the fitting where things get taken apart.

04

Second fitting and finish — 4 to 6 weeks

Rebuilt, embroidered if the commission calls for it, pressed and delivered in a lined box with the pattern reference.
